# Placement OS: Brand and Positioning

Status: pre-launch. Android-first app, preparing a campus pilot. Founder is an individual; there is no registered company and there are no customers, testimonials, statistics or university partners to cite. Demo data in the product is fictional.

## 1. One-line positioning
Placement OS is a student-first campus placement app: build your profile once, see which drives you are eligible for and why, opt in within seconds with prefilled data, and never miss a deadline.

## 2. Audiences

### Students (primary)
- Pains: re-typing the same details for every drive; unclear eligibility; last-minute deadlines; scattered resume and profile links.
- Promise: one profile, clear eligibility reasons, review-and-opt-in, reminders at 60/30/15 minutes.
- Tone toward them: friendly, direct, practical, a bit of campus humour. Never fear-based ("you'll lose your job!").

### Placement officers / TPOs (secondary, decision-makers for a pilot)
- Pains: building drives in spreadsheets and forms; knowing how many students qualify; tracking applicants; reaching the right audience.
- Promise: drive wizard with a completeness check before publishing, eligibility counts (with per-reason counts), an applicant pipeline with an append-only status history, announcements, and officer verification of academic fields.
- Tone toward them: calm, professional, respectful of their workload. Offer a pilot, not a hard sell.

## 3. What we can claim (source of truth)
Student side: profile with academics, links, skills, preferences and up to 5 resume links; field verification status (unverified / student-confirmed / verified); profile completion meter with next step; drives list with search, status and eligibility filters, saved drives; deterministic eligibility check with reasons per criterion (eligible / not eligible / pending); apply preview showing mapped fields, their source and verification, conflicts, missing fields, extra questions and resume choice; idempotent apply (no duplicates); application reference and timeline; withdraw before screening; reminders at 60, 30 and 15 minutes (push and in-app, quiet hours, each can be switched off); notifications.
Officer side: drive wizard with autosaved draft; publish completeness gate; eligible count with per-reason breakdown; applicant list and status pipeline (submitted, screening, assessment, interview, selected, offer issued, offer accepted/declined, rejected, withdrawn, joined); announcements to everyone or a drive's audience; student directory and verification; dashboard (open drives, closing today, applications today, pending verifications).
Platform: Android-first Expo app, Express API. Indian formats (rupee, lakh/LPA, en-IN dates, IST).

## 4. What we must NOT claim
- No ERP, SSO, LMS or university-system integration (not built).
- No numbers: users, placements, universities, time saved, rankings.
- No testimonials, reviews, logos or university or company names.
- No claims about any competitor (including Superset). We compare only on what we build, and only when asked.
- No guarantee of jobs, shortlisting or outcomes.
- Do not say "live" or "available now" before the pilot starts. Use "launching soon", "campus pilot".

## 5. Value propositions
1. Fill once, apply many: profile data prefills each application.
2. Eligibility with reasons: every criterion shown with required versus actual.
3. Review and opt in in seconds: you see what is shared, and you decide.
4. Deadline safety: 60/30/15-minute reminders with quiet hours.
5. Officers: wizard, counts, pipeline, announcements, verification.
6. Student-first design: plain language, tabular numbers for CGPA/CTC/dates, large touch targets, no dark patterns.

## 6. Taglines
Primary: **Apply by reviewing, not typing.**
Alternates:
- Build once. Apply in seconds.
- Know why you're eligible.
- Your placement season, organised.
- Never miss a closing time.
- Placement drives, minus the chaos. (use sparingly)
For officers: **Run the drive. See the pipeline.**
Pre-launch tag line for every asset: **Launching soon | Campus pilot**

## 7. Voice
- Plain, specific, short sentences. Indian-English spellings and formats (organised, lakh, LPA, DD MMM YYYY).
- Honest about stage: we are early and say so.
- Helpful before promotional: teach something in every post.
- Light humour about shared student experiences (the fifth CGPA entry), never about individuals or colleges.
- Hinglish allowed sparingly in reels captions; keep core claims in clear English.

## 8. Do and don't
| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Say "launching soon" and "campus pilot" | Say "trusted by", "used by", "#1", "best" |
| Show illustrative screens labelled as such | Show fake dashboards with invented stats |
| Describe features exactly as built | Mention integrations, AI matching or placements guaranteed |
| Credit that every drive has its own rules | Imply eligibility is a prediction of selection |
| Invite pilot conversations with TPOs | Pressure or guilt-trip students |
| Use brand teal, coral, amber, ink | Use real company or college logos, stock photos |
| Compare on our own features only | Name or disparage incumbents |

## 9. Visual identity
- Colours: primary teal #126B63, dark teal #0D4F4A, coral #F26B4F (accent), amber #F5B544 (highlight pills), ink #172B3A, muted #64748B, canvas #F6F8F9, border #E4E9EC; success #16845B, warning #B7791F, error #C2413A, info #2563A6.
- Type: Inter or system font; bold headlines, regular body; tabular numbers for numbers.
- Logo: rounded-square mark with a check and coral dot, plus wordmark "Placement OS" (see `app-store-assets/icon-512.png`).
- Motion: fade and slide only. No bounce, wobble or shake. Respect Reduce Motion.
- Social template: dark teal cover and CTA, canvas content slides, coral underline accent, footer "Launching soon | Campus pilot".

Safe answer: "Other platforms exist and many campuses already use them. We are a new, student-first app focused on profile-once, eligibility reasons, quick opt-in and deadline reminders. We are piloting soon and would love feedback." Do not claim to be better, cheaper or faster.
