## 4. One-page pitch for TPOs
**Placement OS: a student-first placement app for your cell (campus pilot proposal)**

*The challenge.* Each drive needs rules, forms, eligibility checks, deadline communication and applicant tracking. Students re-enter the same details repeatedly.

*What Placement OS offers.*
- Drive wizard with autosaved drafts and a completeness check before publishing.
- Eligible-student count before publishing, with reasons for those not eligible (CGPA, 10th/12th, backlogs, gap years, department, batch).
- Applicant pipeline with a recorded status history, from submitted to joined.
- Announcements to all students or a single drive's audience.
- Verification of student academic fields by the officer.
- Dashboard: open drives, closing today, applications today, pending verifications.
- Students: profile once, eligibility reasons, prefilled review-and-opt-in, reminders at 60/30/15 minutes.

*Where we are.* Pre-launch; Android-first demo build with fictional data. No university integrations (ERP/SSO) are built, so profiles are entered by students and verified by officers within the app. The founder is an individual; there is no registered company yet.

*Proposed pilot.* One department or one drive cycle, agreed with your cell; weekly feedback call; you can stop at any time. Data handling terms to be agreed in writing before any real student data is used: [FOUNDER TO FINALISE].

*Contact.* [Founder name], [email], [phone].
