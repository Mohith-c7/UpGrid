# Product Demo Script: Student apply flow and officer drive wizard (target 6:00)

Recorded on the demo build with seeded fictional data (fictional companies, demo officer and students). Say once at the start: "This is a demo build with fictional data." Do not mention integrations. Cursor moves smooth, no zoom bounces.

## Part A: Student (0:00-3:30)
| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:20 | Welcome and sign in | "This is UpGrid, a demo build with fictional data. I will walk through what a student does, then what a placement officer does." |
| 0:20-0:55 | Profile tab, completion meter | "Here is the profile. Academics, contact, links, skills and preferences. The meter shows percentage done and the next step. Notice each academic field carries a status: unverified, student-confirmed, or verified." |
| 0:55-1:15 | Resumes screen | "I can save up to five resume links, with labels, and mark one as default." |
| 1:15-1:55 | Opportunities tab, filters | "The Opportunities tab lists drives. I can search, filter by open or closing, filter by eligibility, sort by closing time, newest or package, and save drives for later. Packages show in lakh per annum." |
| 1:55-2:25 | Drive detail, eligibility card | "Open a drive and you see the role, rounds, requirements and an eligibility card. Each rule shows what is required and what my profile says. Pending means a detail is missing." |
| 2:25-3:05 | Apply review screen | "Tap apply. Before anything is sent I see the preview: each prefilled field, its source and verification, any conflicts, and extra questions I need to answer. I choose a resume, accept the declarations, and opt in." |
| 3:05-3:30 | Receipt and Applications tab | "I get a reference number, and the application has a timeline. Until screening starts, I can withdraw. Reminders at 60, 30 and 15 minutes are in Settings, with quiet hours." |

## Part B: Officer (3:30-6:00)
| Time | Screen | Voiceover |
|---|---|---|
| 3:30-3:50 | Officer dashboard | "Now the officer view. The dashboard shows open drives, drives closing today, applications today and pending verifications." |
| 3:50-4:50 | Drive wizard | "To create a drive I use the wizard: company and role, CTC, locations, eligibility rules like minimum CGPA, backlogs, departments and batches, rounds, and custom questions. It autosaves as a draft." |
| 4:50-5:15 | Publish check, eligible count | "Before publishing there is a completeness check that lists anything missing. I can also see how many students are eligible, with the reasons for those who are not." |
| 5:15-5:40 | Applicants and pipeline | "After publishing, applicants appear. I move them through stages: screening, assessment, interview, selected, offer, joined. Every change is recorded." |
| 5:40-6:00 | Announce, close | "I can send an announcement to everyone or to one drive's audience. That is the walkthrough. UpGrid is launching soon with a campus pilot. Link below." |

Note for editors: if a screen differs in the final build, re-record that segment; do not narrate features that are not visible.
