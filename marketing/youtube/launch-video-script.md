# Launch Video Script: "Introducing UpGrid" (target 3:30)

Format: founder voiceover plus screen recordings from the demo build (fictional data, labelled "Illustrative"). Brand colours, fade/slide transitions only. No music with unclear licence. No stats, testimonials, university or company names, or integration claims.

| Time | Visual | Voiceover | On-screen text |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:12 | Founder to camera (or title card) | "Placement season. Same details, different form, every single drive. I think there is a better way, and I have been building it." | UpGrid: Launching soon |
| 0:12-0:35 | Quick cuts of forms, spreadsheets, a phone clock | "If you have been through campus placements, you know the pattern. You type your CGPA, your 10th and 12th percentages, your backlogs, your links. Then you do it again. And somewhere in between you wonder: am I even eligible for this?" | The same details. Every drive. |
| 0:35-0:55 | Founder to camera | "I am an independent founder. There is no company behind this yet, no customer list, no big numbers to show. Just a product, and a campus pilot we are preparing. I would rather tell you that than make something up." | Independent founder. Pre-launch. |
| 0:55-1:30 | Screen: profile screen, completion meter, resume links | "UpGrid is a student-first app. You build your profile once: academics, links, skills, your role and location preferences, and up to five resume links. A completion meter tells you what is missing and what to do next. Each academic field shows whether it is unverified, student-confirmed, or verified by your placement officer." | Build once. Fields labelled. |
| 1:30-2:05 | Screen: drives list, eligibility reasons | "Then you see drives. For each one, the app checks your profile against the drive's rules and shows the reason, criterion by criterion: required versus yours. Eligible, not eligible, or pending when something is missing. No guessing, no silent rejection." | Eligible / Not eligible / Pending |
| 2:05-2:40 | Screen: apply preview and receipt | "Applying is a review, not a typing session. You see what will be shared and where each value came from. Opt in. Done. |
| 2:40-3:00 | Screen: reminder notification, settings | "And deadlines. Reminders arrive 60, 30 and 15 minutes before a drive closes, by push and in the app. You can switch any off and set quiet hours. Once you have applied, they stop." | 60 / 30 / 15 minutes |
