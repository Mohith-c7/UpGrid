# Instagram Reels scripts (13)

Rules for all reels: hook lands in the first 2 seconds; 20 to 35 seconds; fade/slide text only (no bounce or shake); app footage must be from the demo build with fictional data and labelled 'Illustrative'; no stats, testimonials or integrations; end card shows 'Launching soon | Campus pilot'. Use original, royalty-free or no music. Captions end with hashtags.

## Reel 1: Same form, fifth time

- **Hook (0 to 2s):** The fifth time you type your CGPA this week... (spoken and shown as big on-screen text)
- **Shot list:** Eyes on a laptop, hand on forehead; phone with a form; close-up of fingers typing; end card
- **On-screen text:** Text 1: 'CGPA. Again.' Text 2: 'There has to be a better way.' Text 3: 'Build your profile once.'
- **Voiceover (about 25s):** "The fifth time you type your CGPA this week... Every placement drive asks for the same details. Imagine filling them once, and just reviewing them next time. That is what we are building with UpGrid. Launching soon, campus pilot."
- **CTA (last 3s):** Join the waitlist, link in bio.
- **Caption:** Tired of typing the same details for every drive? We are building UpGrid so you fill your profile once. Launching soon. Campus pilot.
- **Hashtags:** #UpGrid #CampusPlacements #PlacementSeason #PlacementPrep #StudentLife #LaunchingSoon
