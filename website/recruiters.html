For recruiters

Interview, score and decide in one workspace.

UpGrid gives company teams a focused workspace: candidates who applied, structured scorecards, shortlists and offers, plus optional AI that drafts interview questions for you to review.

Join the campus pilot

A calmer campus hiring process.

Candidates who applied

See each applicant with the details they agreed to share on that application, and nothing beyond it.

Scorecards

Rate every round on the same criteria and record a recommendation, so panels compare like with like.

Rounds and shortlists

Move candidates through the rounds you defined for the drive, with a permanent status history.

Company teams

Invite colleagues to your company workspace so the whole panel sees the same pipeline.

Interview question drafts

Generate suggested questions from the candidate’s shared resume and the job description. You edit and approve; nothing is sent automatically.

Scheduling

Interviews and tests are scheduled through the placement cell, and candidates are notified in the app.

Guardrails

Your judgement stays in charge.

  • AI questions are drafts. You review them before use.
  • AI does not rank, shortlist or reject candidates.
  • Only the resume and job description are used, not the rest of the candidate’s profile.
  • Scorecards are visible to your hiring team and the placement cell, not to other companies.
