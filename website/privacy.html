1. Who we are

UpGrid is an early-stage project run by an independent founder: [FOUNDER FULL NAME], [CITY, INDIA]. There is no registered company at this time. For the purposes of this policy the founder acts as the data fiduciary for the website and, for the app, together with the college that runs a pilot [ROLES TO BE CONFIRMED BY LAWYER]. Contact: hello@upgrid.example.

2. What this covers

This policy covers this website (including the waitlist and contact pages) and, once the pilot starts, the UpGrid app.

3. Website and waitlist

  • If you join the waitlist we store the name, role, college (optional), email and mobile number (optional) you give us, the time you consented, and a hashed form of your network address used to limit spam.
  • We use this only to email you about the pilot and launch, and to answer you. We plan to send at most three emails: a confirmation, one update when the pilot opens, and one feedback question.
  • This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics or advertising trackers at present. If that changes we will update this policy first.

4. App data

  • Account: name, email, mobile number, password (stored hashed).
  • Profile: academics, links, skills, preferences, resume links.
  • Activity: applications and status history, test answers and scores, code submissions, notifications, support tickets, push token.
  • AI: only if you opt in; the text needed for your request is processed by a third-party model provider.

5. Why we use it, and on what basis

To operate the placement features you ask for, on the basis of your consent and, where applicable, your college’s legitimate placement purposes [LAWYER TO CONFIRM LAWFUL BASES UNDER THE DPDP ACT, 2023].

6. Who we share it with

  • Your campus placement officers, for your campus.
  • A recruiter, limited to what you choose to share when you apply.
  • Service providers that run the product: hosting [PROVIDER], email delivery [PROVIDER], push notifications (Expo), code execution service [PROVIDER], and, if AI is on, the AI model provider [PROVIDER].
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

We do not sell personal data.

7. Retention and deletion

Waitlist data is kept until the pilot ends or you ask us to remove it [PERIOD TO BE DECIDED]. App data is kept while your account is active. You can delete your account in the app. [RETENTION AND PERMANENT-ERASURE TIMELINES TO BE DECIDED.]

8. Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or erase your data, withdraw consent, and raise a grievance, by emailing us. We will respond within [TIMEFRAME TO BE DECIDED]. [GRIEVANCE OFFICER DETAILS TO BE ADDED.]

9. Security

We use role-based access, separation of campus data, hashed passwords, rate limits and an audit trail for sensitive changes. No system is perfectly secure. A review against the DPDP Act, 2023 is pending.

10. Children

UpGrid is meant for college students. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian and your college before using it [LAWYER TO REVIEW DPDP CHILDREN PROVISIONS].
