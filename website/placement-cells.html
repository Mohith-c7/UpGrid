Less spreadsheet. More certainty.

Drive wizard

Autosaved drafts and a completeness check before publishing. Compensation must be stated; if a breakdown is missing, students see “Breakdown not provided”.

Eligible counts, with reasons

Before you publish, see how many students qualify and how many are excluded by CGPA, backlogs, department, batch and other rules.

Applicant pipeline

Move applicants from submitted through screening, assessment, interview, offer and joined. Every change is recorded and cannot be edited later.

Scheduling in bulk

Schedule interviews or tests for up to 200 applicants at once, online or in person. Students are notified and reminded.

Announcements

Message every student, or only the audience of a single drive.

Verification

Review and verify student academic fields. Editing a verified field resets it, so what you verified stays accurate.

CSV import and export

Import student records in batches matched by registration number, with a report of what was updated, unmatched or invalid. Export applicants to a CSV that is safe to open in a spreadsheet.

Analytics

A dashboard of open drives, closing today, applications and pending verifications, with deeper placement analytics being finished for the pilot.

Policy rules and restrictions

Set campus placement policy rules and restrictions so they apply consistently instead of by memory. Being finished for the pilot.

A pilot, not a hard sell

We would like your feedback first.

UpGrid is pre-launch. We are looking for a few placement cells to shape a small pilot: one department or one drive cycle, agreed with you, with a weekly feedback call. You can stop at any time.

  • No integration with your ERP or login is needed or built; students enter profiles and you verify them.
  • Officer accounts need an invite code that you control.
  • Data handling terms are agreed in writing before any real student data is used.
  • Pricing is not decided, and none is published here.
