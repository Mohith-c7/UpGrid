Three roles, one app

Everyone in the placement season works from the same facts.

Students

Know where you stand before you apply.

  • Fill your academics, links, skills and resumes once. Every drive prefills from it.
  • See each drive rule next to your own value, so you know why you are eligible, not eligible or pending.
  • Review what is shared, then opt in. No re-typing.
  • Get reminders 60, 30 and 15 minutes before a deadline, with quiet hours.
See the student experience

Eligible means you meet a drive’s listed rules. It never predicts selection.

Placement cells

Run the drive. See the pipeline.

  • A drive wizard with autosaved drafts and a completeness check before you publish.
  • See how many students are eligible, and why the others are not, before you publish.
  • Move applicants through a pipeline with a permanent status history; schedule interviews and tests in bulk.
  • Announcements, student verification, CSV import, analytics and policy rules.
See the placement cell tools

No ERP or single sign-on integration is built. Profiles are entered by students and verified by officers inside the app.

Recruiters

Interview, score and decide in one workspace.

  • See the candidates who applied, with only the details they agreed to share.
  • Record structured scorecards for every round.
  • Draft interview questions from a candidate’s shared resume and the job description. You review and edit before using them.
  • Track shortlists and offers with your hiring team.

What is in the app

Everything between “a drive is announced” and “the offer is accepted”.

Profile once

Academics, links, skills, preferences and up to five resume links. A completion meter tells you the next step.

Eligibility with reasons

Every rule shown as required versus yours: eligible, not eligible or pending. Deterministic, never a guess.

Review and opt in

See what will be shared, with its source and verification status, answer extra questions, choose a resume and apply once.

60/30/15 reminders

Push and in-app reminders before a deadline closes. Quiet hours, and each reminder can be switched off.

Interviews and tests, scheduled

Placement cells schedule assessments and interviews, singly or in bulk. Students get the details and a reminder.

Online tests

MCQ and coding tests with a timer, a question palette and automatic evaluation.

Recruiter workspace

Rounds, shortlists, scorecards and offers for company teams, limited to what candidates agreed to share.

AI assistant, opt-in

Help with preparation for students and question drafts for recruiters. It never decides eligibility or selection.

Analytics and policy

Dashboards for placement cells, plus campus policy rules and restrictions applied consistently.

CSV import and export

Import student records in batches and export applicants to a spreadsheet-safe CSV.

Support tickets

Students raise questions from inside the app; placement officers reply and resolve them.

Consent and access control

Role-based access, consent at sign-up, and a share preview before every application.

Where we are: pre-launch. Profile, eligibility, applications, reminders and the placement cell tools are in the demo build. Tests, the recruiter workspace, AI and analytics are still being finished, so the pilot will switch them on in stages.

How it works

Four steps from drive to decision.

  1. Set up once

    Students build one profile. Placement cells import student records by CSV and verify academic fields.

  2. Publish a drive

    The cell uses the wizard, sees the eligible count and why others are excluded, then publishes.

  3. Review and opt in

    Students see their eligibility reasons, review the share preview and apply. Reminders protect the deadline.

  4. Test, interview, decide

    Tests and interviews are scheduled, recruiters score candidates, and every status change is recorded.

Assessments and coding tests

Run the test inside the same app.

Placement cells can set MCQ and coding tests for a drive. Students take them on their phone with a visible timer and a question palette. Scores are calculated by the server, not the phone.

  • MCQ auto-evaluation with a result as soon as the test closes.
  • Coding questions run against test cases in an isolated execution service, never on the app server.
  • Light-touch integrity signals for staff to review. No camera or microphone recording.

Security and privacy

You decide what is shared. We say plainly what is not done yet.

Students see exactly what an application shares before they submit. Access follows the role: students see their own data, officers see their own campus, recruiters see what candidates agree to share.

Questions

Straight answers.

Is Placement OS live?

Not yet. We are launching soon, starting with a campus pilot. You can join the waitlist and we will email you when the pilot opens.

Who is behind it?

An independent founder. There is no registered company yet, and no customers or partner colleges to name.

Does being eligible mean I will be selected?

No. Eligible only means you meet the drive’s listed criteria. Selection is decided by the recruiter and the placement cell, never by the app or by AI.

Does it integrate with my college ERP or login?

No. Integrations are not built. Students enter their profile in the app and placement officers verify it.

Who sees my data?

The apply screen shows what will be shared before you submit. Your placement cell can see your profile and applications on your campus; a recruiter sees only what that application shares. Details are on the security page.

What does the AI do, and can I switch it off?

It is optional and opt-in for students. It can help you prepare, and it can draft interview questions for recruiters. It never decides eligibility or selection, and drafts need human review. See AI principles.

Is it free? What does it cost?

Pricing has not been decided and we are not publishing any until it is confirmed. The campus pilot is by agreement with each placement cell.

Which phones does it run on?

Android first. A Play Store link will be added when the pilot build is listed.
