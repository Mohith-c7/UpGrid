For students

Know why you are eligible. Apply in seconds.

Build your profile once. UpGrid shows every drive rule next to your own value, lets you review what is shared, and reminds you before the deadline.

The season, without the fifth CGPA entry.

Profile once

Academics, links, skills, preferences and up to five resume links, with a completion meter and a clear next step.

Verified where it counts

Each academic field shows whether it is unverified, confirmed by you, or verified by your placement officer.

Eligibility with reasons

Eligible, not eligible or pending, with every criterion shown as required versus yours. Pending means a detail is missing from your profile.

Find the right drives

Search, filter by status and eligibility, save drives for later. Packages in lakh per annum and dates in IST.

Never miss a closing time

Reminders 60, 30 and 15 minutes before a deadline, by push and in-app. Quiet hours supported; each reminder can be switched off.

Interview and test schedules

Your schedule and venue or link appear in the app, with a reminder before it starts.

Review and opt in

You see it all before you apply.

The apply screen lists every field that will be shared, where it came from and whether it is verified. You answer any extra questions, pick a resume and confirm.

  • Conflicts and missing fields are flagged before you submit.
  • Applying is safe against double taps: no duplicate applications.
  • You get a reference number and a timeline of status changes.
  • You can withdraw before your application reaches screening.
  • If you get an offer, you accept or decline inside the app.
