Security and privacy

What we collect, who sees it, and what is not done yet.

A plain-language summary. The full privacy policy and terms are drafts awaiting legal review, and we say so wherever it matters.

Data we collect and who sees it

DataWhyWho can see it
Account: name, email, mobile numberTo sign you in and contact youYou; your placement cell; your mobile number is not shown to recruiters unless a feature says so
Profile: academics, links, skills, preferences, resume linksTo compute eligibility and prefill applicationsYou and your campus placement officers. A recruiter sees only the fields shown in the apply preview
Applications and status historyTo run the drive pipelineYou, your placement cell, and the recruiter for that drive
Test answers, scores, code submissionsTo evaluate assessmentsYou, the placement cell, and the recruiter if the cell shares results
Notifications and push tokenTo send reminders and updatesYou; the push token is used only to deliver your notifications
Support ticketsTo answer your questionsYou and your placement officers
AI requests (only if you opt in)To produce the help you ask forYou, and the third-party AI provider that processes the request
Website waitlist: name, role, college, email, optional mobileTo email you about the pilotThe founder only

Consent

You accept the terms and privacy policy when you create an account. Each application shows exactly what will be shared before you submit. The student AI assistant is opt-in.

Deletion

You can delete your account from the app. We are finalising retention timelines and the process for permanently removing data, and will publish them in the final privacy policy. Waitlist entries can be removed by emailing us.

How access is controlled

Role-based access (student, placement officer, recruiter), data separated per campus, short-lived sign-in tokens, hashed passwords, rate limits, and an audit trail for sensitive changes.

What the website does

This website sets no cookies and has no analytics or advertising trackers at present. The waitlist form sends only what you type, with your consent, and a hashed network address used to limit spam.

DPDP review is pending. We have not yet completed a review against India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and its rules. Until that is done and written data-handling terms are agreed with a campus, we will not use real student data in a pilot. We are not claiming any certification or audit.

Not done yet: no ERP, SSO or LMS integration; no independent security audit; encryption at rest depends on the hosting provider and will be confirmed in the final policy. Report a security concern to hello@placementos.example.
