Our guardrails

AI never decides eligibility or selection

Eligibility comes from fixed, visible rules. Shortlisting, selection and offers are made by people.

Drafts need human review

AI output is a draft. It is not sent, shared or acted on until a person approves it.

Opt-in for students

The student AI assistant is off until you turn it on, and you can stop using it at any time.

Minimal data

Only what a feature needs is sent to the model, for example a resume and a job description for question drafts. Not your whole profile.

Quotas

Each user has a daily limit to prevent misuse and runaway use.

Off by default if not configured

If the AI service is not set up, the AI features are simply switched off.

What the AI can do

Student assistant

Answers preparation questions and explains how the app works. Opt-in.

Resume review

Suggests clearer wording and structure for a resume you choose to share with it. You decide what to change.

Mock interview practice

Practice questions and feedback for your own preparation. Not shared with recruiters or the placement cell.

Interview question drafts for recruiters

Questions generated from a candidate’s shared resume and the job description, for the recruiter to edit and approve.

Third party processing. When AI features are on, the text needed for a request is sent to a third-party model provider (currently Google’s Gemini API) and is not used to decide anything about you. The final privacy policy will name providers and retention; it is still a draft. AI answers can be wrong, so check them.

AI features are launching last and may be switched off for part of the pilot.
